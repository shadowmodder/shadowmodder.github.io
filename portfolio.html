vllm-project/vllm · #66473
[Bugfix][Multimodal] Domain-separate content digests and caller uuids in mm hashes
get_mm_hashes() emitted content digests and caller-chosen uuids into one untagged keyspace, so a caller could poison the multimodal processor and encoder caches for later callers by setting uuid to a target item's predictable digest. Fix: prefix every hash with its source domain (content: / uuid:) at the single choke point.

weaviate/weaviate-python-client · #2262
fix(batch): stop a stale stream sender from stealing requests after a fast reconnect
After a hang-up, if the reconnect finished inside the old sender's 1s queue wait, the stale sender could wake, take the next request, and drop it into the dead stream — losing the batch silently and wedging every later add_object. A stream generation counter makes stale senders hand requests back to the live stream. Fixed in both the sync and async clients.

One object that failed client-side encoding aborted the whole batch — and in streaming mode killed the send loop, silently dropping every unsent object. Objects are now encoded one by one; a failure is recorded as that object's own error, naming its UUID, and the rest of the batch is sent.

vllm-project/vllm · #67643
[Bugfix][Compilation] Remove AllReduceFusionPass.__del__ destroying the shared FlashInfer workspace
The fusion pass's __del__ destroyed the process-global FlashInfer all-reduce workspace on garbage collection — while compiled callables, CUDA graphs, and communicators could still be using it. Workspace lifetime is owned by the process and the communicator (destroy is atexit-registered), so the pass-level destructor is removed, with a behavioral regression test pinning that the pass does not own the workspace.
